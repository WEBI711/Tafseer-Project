TAFSEER — DESIGN CONCEPTS
=========================

HOW TO VIEW
-----------
1. Open this folder
2. Double-click  index.html
   (it will open in your web browser — Chrome, Safari or Edge)
3. Click any of the five design cards

Inside each design you can click the tabs at the top:
    READ  ·  BROWSE  ·  SEARCH  ·  CHAT
Every design shows all four, in its own visual style.


WHAT YOU ARE LOOKING AT
-----------------------
Five different visual directions for the same product:
a Qur'an study app that combines reading, browsing, searching
and an AI chat that answers from the tafsir texts.

All five use the same sample content, so they can be compared
side by side.


THE FIVE DIRECTIONS
-------------------
01  Manuscript     Warm parchment, classical serif, ornamental
02  Swiss          Strict grid, monochrome, editorial clarity
03  Marginalia     Quiet reading view, notes in the margin
04  Night Study    Dark, low-contrast, amber accents
05  Workspace      The full application shell

Design 05 is the closest to the finished product shape.


TWO NOTES
---------
- Text content is placeholder. The commentary shown is illustrative,
  not the final published wording.
- The pages load their typefaces from the internet. If you are
  offline they will still work, using standard system fonts.
