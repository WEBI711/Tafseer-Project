SURAH 01 — MAKKI

Al-Fatihah

الفاتحة

A prayer taught by Allah to those who seek guidance — placed at the threshold of the Book, recited in every unit of prayer.

01 The Basmalah

1:1

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

In the name of Allah, the Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate.

Every matter of consequence begins with His name. The Basmalah brings the act under His protection and opens the door to blessing.

1:2

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

All praise and thanks belong to Allah, Lord of the entire universe.

Praise precedes request. The surah is structured as a du'a, and its order is itself instruction: recognise, then ask.

Root Al-Hamd — praise joined with gratitude, expressed by tongue, heart and limb.
1:3—1:4

الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ

The Most Merciful, the Most Compassionate. Master of the Day of Judgement.

Mercy is named before mastery. The One who will judge is first introduced as the One who nurtures.

Why does Al-Fatihah open with praise before making a request?

The surah is structured as a du'a, and the order is deliberate: it establishes the posture before the petition. Praise, then declaration, then request — so the ask emerges from recognition rather than demand.

1:2 "The nature of this surah is that of a du'a — a prayer that begins with the praise…" Taleem al-Quran · para 2
1:5 "This is our declaration to Allah to worship Him alone and turn for help to Him alone." Taleem al-Quran · para 1
