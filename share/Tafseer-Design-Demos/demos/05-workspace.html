Why does Al-Fatihah open with praise before making a request?

The surah is structured as a du'a, and the order is deliberate: it establishes the correct posture before asking. Praise and gratitude come first, then declaration of worship, and only then the request for guidance — so the ask emerges from recognition rather than demand.
